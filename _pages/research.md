## 3D digitization of Yellowstone wolf skulls

### Project Description

Yellowstone National Park is home to one of the best-studied wolf populations in the world. Many of the skulls belonging to individual wolves that were studied since the reintroductions in 1995 and 1996 are housed at the Yellowstone Heritage and Research Center in Gardiner, MT. This museum collection is available to the public, however there is no formal gallery space for them, and Park visitors are required to make appointments in advance to tour of the collection. 3D digitization of this collection is one of the best ways to make it more accessible to the public on a global scale for research, education, and outreach. With an Emerging Conservationists Grant from Conservation Nation and support from the Yellowstone Wolf Project, Maddy started this project with her colleague, Jonathan Keller. The final goal is to create a complete digital repository of the Yellowstone wolf skull collection that will be publicly accessible on MorphoSource. The 3D skull above, for example, is a digitized version of the cranium of 302M, famously nicknamed "Cassanova". This wolf was featured in the PBS documentary *In the Valley of the Wolves* and the National Geographic documentary *Rise of Black Wolf*.
